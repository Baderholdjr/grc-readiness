# {{title}}

## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}}, as a controller, responds to subpoenas, warrants, court orders and similar requests from courts, law enforcement and other government bodies for personal information (PR52).

## Receiving a request

Any employee who receives such a request, in any form, sends it at once to [[TODO: legal or privacy contact]] and does not answer it or disclose information themselves (PR52). Informal requests, such as a phone call from someone claiming to be police, are handled the same way.

The request is logged with the date, the requester, the legal instrument and the information sought.

## Reviewing and responding

[[TODO: legal or privacy lead]] reviews each request before any disclosure (PR52):

- confirm the requester's identity and authority;
- confirm the request is valid and that the law compels {{client}} to respond, naming the law;
- check the scope and disclose only the information the request requires;
- push back on, or seek to narrow, a request that is too broad or lacks proper authority, taking advice from [[TODO: external counsel, if used]];
- decide whether the person concerned can be told, and tell them unless the law or the request forbids it.

Disclosure is approved by [[TODO: approver]] and sent securely.

Where the request concerns information {{client}} holds as a processor for a customer, the customer is told before any disclosure unless the law forbids it.

In an emergency that threatens someone's life, health or security, the reviewer may act faster, but still records the request, the basis and what was disclosed as soon as possible.

Disclosures made under this procedure are compelled by law; they are recorded in the disclosure and transfer register as such, with the law named, unless {{client}} is bound to keep it confidential.
