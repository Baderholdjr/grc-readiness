## Correction requests

People can challenge the accuracy of their information and have it corrected (PR29). Requests can be made through [[TODO: channels]] and are handled under the Access and Correction Request Procedure:

- the person's identity is confirmed;
- the challenged information is checked;
- if it is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date, it is corrected in every system that holds it;
- the person is told the outcome, and the reason if a correction is refused.

Each request is logged with its dates and outcome (PR29).

## Passing on corrections

When {{client}} corrects information that matters to its purposes after it was transferred to processors, agents or service providers, it sends the correction to them and they apply it (PR30). This applies when {{client}} uses processors; if it does not, PR30 is marked not applicable and the reason is recorded. Processor contracts require them to apply corrections within [[TODO: time]].

When such a correction is made after the information was disclosed to third parties, {{client}} tells the third parties that received it (PR31). This applies when {{client}} discloses information to third parties; if it does not, PR31 is marked not applicable and the reason is recorded.

The disclosure and transfer register shows who holds each type of information, so recipients can be found. Corrections passed on are logged with the recipient and date.
