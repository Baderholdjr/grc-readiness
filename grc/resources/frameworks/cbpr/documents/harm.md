## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}}, as a controller, identifies sensitive information and children's information, protects it, and assesses and reduces the risk that personal information is misused in ways that harm people.

## Assessing and mitigating risk

{{client}} identifies and assesses the risk that personal information it holds is misused and causes harm to people, and reduces that risk in proportion to how likely and how severe the harm would be (PR4).

An assessment is done:

- before a new processing activity, product or system that handles personal information goes live;
- when an existing activity changes its purpose, data, recipients or technology;
- for all activities at least [[TODO: frequency, e.g. every year]].

Each assessment considers:

- the types of information, including sensitive and children's information;
- the number of people affected and their relationship with {{client}};
- the ways misuse could happen: unauthorised access, use beyond the stated purposes, unexpected disclosure, inaccuracy;
- the harm that could follow: financial loss, discrimination, physical risk, distress, damage to reputation;
- existing safeguards and their weaknesses.

The assessor rates likelihood and severity, chooses mitigations that fit the rating, and assigns an owner and a due date to each. The assessment and its mitigations are recorded in [[TODO: risk register or tool]] and tracked to closure (PR4). The responsible person approves any residual risk that stays high.
