## Purpose

This policy sets out how {{client}}, as a controller, runs its privacy program so that it meets the Global CBPR program requirements and can show an Accountability Agent that it does. It is the top-level document; the procedures listed under Privacy principles carry the detail.

## Scope

The program covers all personal information {{client}} collects, holds, uses, discloses or transfers for its own purposes, online and offline, in every economy where it operates.

{{scope_statement}}

It applies to all employees, contractors and temporary staff, and, through contracts, to processors, agents and service providers acting for {{client}}.

## Records of processing

{{client}} keeps a record of its processing activities (PR46). For each activity the record shows:

- the purpose of processing;
- the types of personal information and of individuals;
- where the information comes from;
- who it is disclosed or transferred to, including processors, and to which economies;
- how long it is kept;
- whether it includes sensitive or children's information;
- the safeguards that protect it.
