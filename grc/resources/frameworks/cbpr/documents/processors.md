## Security obligations

By contract or other reasonable means, processors must (PR42):

- keep a security program proportionate to the sensitivity of the information and the services they provide (42a);
- notify {{client}} promptly when they become aware of a breach of the information: within [[TODO: time]] (42b);
- take immediate steps to fix the cause of the breach (42c).
