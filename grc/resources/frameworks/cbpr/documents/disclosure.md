# {{title}}

## Purpose

This procedure sets when {{client}}, as a controller, discloses personal information to other controllers or transfers it to processors, and how it shows each disclosure or transfer serves a proper purpose.

Each section applies only when {{client}} makes that kind of disclosure or transfer. Where one does not apply, the requirement is marked not applicable and the reason is recorded.

## Disclosures to controllers

This section applies when {{client}} discloses personal information to other controllers, such as [[TODO: e.g. business partners, insurers, group companies]] (PR15).

Disclosures to other controllers are made only:

- to fulfil the original purpose of collection, or a compatible or related purpose; or
- with the person's express consent where the disclosure is needed to provide a product or service they asked for; or
- when the law compels it (PR15).

For each disclosure {{client}} records, in the disclosure and transfer register (PR15, PR17):

- the recipient and its economy;
- each data type disclosed;
- the purpose it was collected for;
- how the disclosure fulfils that purpose, for example "delivery address shared with the courier partner to deliver the order";
- the agreement that governs it.

## Transfers to processors

This section applies when {{client}} uses processors, agents or service providers to handle personal information for it (PR16). Transfers to them follow the same rules as disclosures to controllers: they serve the original or a compatible purpose, and each one is recorded in the register with the data types, purpose and how the transfer fulfils it (PR16, PR17).

Processors are chosen, contracted and monitored under the Processor Management Procedure.

## Disclosures for other purposes

A disclosure or transfer for any purpose other than the original or a compatible one is made only when (PR18):

- the person has given express consent (18a);
- it is necessary to provide a product or service the person asked for (18b), and the necessity is recorded; or
- the law compels it (18c). The law is named and the reason it applies is recorded, unless {{client}} is bound to keep it confidential, so the Accountability Agent can verify it.

Before any such disclosure, [[TODO: privacy lead]] approves it and records which ground applies (PR17, PR18). Requests from courts, regulators and law enforcement follow the Legal and Government Request Procedure.

## Where due diligence is impractical

Sometimes {{client}} discloses information to recipients whose practices it cannot reasonably check, for example [[TODO: e.g. a large public payment network or a government body]]. This section applies only when such disclosures happen (PR57).

For each type of such disclosure, {{client}} records (PR57):

- why due diligence is impractical or impossible;
- how the information is otherwise protected, such as by limiting what is disclosed, by encryption, or by the recipient's legal obligations;
- any consent obtained from the person and how.

Disclosures required by the domestic law of an economy where {{client}} operates do not need due diligence or consent under this section; the law relied on is still recorded (PR57).
