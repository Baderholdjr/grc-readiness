## Notice at collection

Wherever information is collected, by {{client}} directly or by a third party collecting for it, people are told that it is being collected, at the time or, where that is not practical, as soon as practicable afterwards (PR7). Collection points include [[TODO: e.g. web forms, app sign-up, call centre script, paper forms, events]].

The notice at collection explains, in writing or orally, the purposes the information is collected for (PR8). Short notices link or refer to the full privacy statement.

When information will or may be shared with third parties, the notice at collection also says so and why (PR9). This applies only where {{client}} shares information with third parties; if it does not, PR9 is marked not applicable and the reason is recorded.

Third parties collecting for {{client}} are instructed by contract to give the same notice. A register of collection points and their notices is kept at [[TODO: location]].

## Qualifications

Notice at or before collection may not be needed in limited cases. {{client}} relies only on the ones that fit, and each one relied on is recorded with the collection point it covers and its justification, for the Accountability Agent to verify (PR6, PR7, PR8, PR9):

- obviousness: the person gives the information in a way that makes the purpose plain, such as handing over a business card;
- publicly available information;
- technological impracticability: information collected automatically, such as through cookies, where notice is given as soon as practicable afterwards;
- a lawful government request where notice would prejudice an investigation;
- lawful process, such as civil discovery;
- information received from a third party, where the recipient is not the one collecting it;
- a legitimate investigation of a breach of contract, a code of conduct or the law, where notice would compromise it;
- an emergency that threatens someone's life, health or security.
