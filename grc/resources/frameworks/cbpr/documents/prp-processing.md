## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}}, as a processor, handles personal information on behalf of its customers, who are the controllers: keeping to their purposes and instructions, acting on their requests, and handling privacy requests, complaints and legal requests that concern their information.

## Controller instructions

{{client}} processes a controller's personal information only for the purposes that controller specifies (PRP9). The purposes are set in [[TODO: e.g. the customer agreement and data processing addendum]] and any further written instructions.

To make sure instructions are followed (PRP11):

- each controller's instructions are recorded in [[TODO: instruction register or contract system]] and linked to the services and systems that process its information;
- staff with access are told which instructions apply, and access is limited to the controllers and systems their role needs;
- systems are configured to keep each controller's information separate and to the agreed processing;
- a new type of processing for a controller starts only after [[TODO: account owner or privacy lead]] confirms it is within the instructions;
- if {{client}} believes an instruction cannot be followed or would breach the law, it tells the controller promptly and does not act on it until resolved;
- compliance with instructions is checked at least [[TODO: frequency]], and gaps are fixed.

{{client}} does not use a controller's information for its own purposes.

## Deletion and correction on request

When a controller asks, {{client}} deletes, updates or corrects personal information it holds for that controller, where necessary and appropriate (PRP10):

- requests come from the controller's authorised contacts through [[TODO: channel, e.g. support portal, API, account manager]];
- the request is logged and carried out within [[TODO: time, or "the time set in the agreement"]];
- the change is applied in every system and sub-processor holding the information;
- {{client}} confirms completion to the controller.

Where a controller can make the change itself through the service, {{client}} explains how.

## Requests and complaints

{{client}} has procedures to handle privacy requests and complaints about controllers' information, or to pass them to the controller (PRP13):

- a request or complaint from an individual about a controller's information is forwarded to that controller within [[TODO: time]], unless the controller's agreement says {{client}} answers it;
- {{client}} does not answer the individual on the substance unless the controller instructs it;
- {{client}} helps the controller respond, for example by finding, exporting, correcting or deleting the information;
- a complaint about {{client}}'s own conduct is investigated by the responsible person and the controller is told.

Each request or complaint is logged with dates and actions (PRP13).

## Legal requests

If {{client}} receives a subpoena, warrant, court order or similar demand for a controller's personal information, it tells that controller before disclosing, unless the law forbids it (PRP14). The request goes at once to [[TODO: legal contact]], who checks it is valid, discloses only what is compelled, and records the request, the decision and the notice given to the controller.

Employees likely to receive such requests are trained on this procedure, and completion is recorded (PRP14).
