## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}}, as a controller, limits the personal information it collects to what its purposes need, collects it lawfully and fairly, and uses it only for the purposes people were told about.

## Sources of personal information

{{client}} identifies every way it obtains personal information and records it in the record of processing (PR10):

- directly from the person, such as through forms, accounts, calls and correspondence;
- from third parties collecting it for {{client}}, such as [[TODO: e.g. resellers, marketing agencies, event organisers]];
- from other sources, such as [[TODO: e.g. public registers, data brokers, business partners, referrals]].

For each source the record states the practices that apply: what is collected, how, the notice given and, for third parties, the agreement that governs the collection (PR10). A new source is added before information from it is used.

## Collecting only what is relevant

{{client}} collects only personal information that is relevant to the purposes stated in its privacy statement or at collection, or to compatible or related purposes (PR11).

- Each data type in the record of processing is linked to the purposes and uses it serves.
- Where a use is compatible with or related to the original purpose rather than the same, the record gives the reason it is compatible (PR11).
- New forms, fields and data feeds are reviewed by [[TODO: privacy lead or delegate]] before launch; a field without a stated purpose is removed or made optional.
- Collection points are reviewed at least [[TODO: frequency, e.g. every year]] to remove data no longer needed.

## Lawful and fair collection

Personal information is collected lawfully and fairly, under the law of each economy where {{client}} collects it: [[TODO: governing laws]] (PR12). In particular:

- no collection by deception, such as hidden fields, misleading forms or pretending to be someone else;
- no collection that breaches the terms on which a source obtained the information;
- third parties collecting for {{client}} are bound by contract to collect lawfully and fairly, and their practices are checked when they are engaged (PR12).

## Using information for its purposes

{{client}} uses personal information only for the purposes stated in its privacy statement or at collection, or for compatible or related purposes (PR13). To keep to this:

- each use is recorded against its purpose in the record of processing;
- a proposed new use is checked by the privacy lead against the stated purposes before it starts; the decision and reasons are recorded;
- if the new use is compatible or related, the record says why; if it is not, the section below applies;
- staff are told, through training and system access, which uses are allowed for the information they handle (PR13).

## Unrelated uses

This section applies only if {{client}} uses personal information for a purpose unrelated to the one it was collected for. If it does not, PR14 is marked not applicable and the reason is recorded.

{{client}} uses information for an unrelated purpose only when (PR14):

- the person has given express consent, obtained as the Choice and Consent Procedure describes, for example online at collection, by email, through a preference page, by phone or by post; or
- the law requires it, in which case the law is named and recorded.

Consent records show who consented, to what, when and how. Where consent is withdrawn, the unrelated use stops.
