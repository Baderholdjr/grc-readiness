## Correction and deletion

People can challenge the accuracy of their information and ask for it to be corrected, completed, amended or deleted (PR45):

- the request mechanism is clear and conspicuous (PR45a);
- where appropriate, the information is corrected, completed, amended or deleted (PR45b);
- this is done within a reasonable time: [[TODO: response time]] (PR45c);
- the person gets a copy of the corrected information or confirmation that the change or deletion was made (PR45d);
- where a correction is passed to processors or third parties, the Data Quality and Correction Procedure applies.

## Refusals and Qualifications

If {{client}} refuses a request in whole or in part, it tells the person why and how to challenge the decision, including whom to contact (PR44, PR45e).

A refusal or limit may rest only on a Qualification:

- the burden or expense would be unreasonable or disproportionate to the risk to the person's privacy, for example repetitive or vexatious requests;
- confidentiality, legal or security reasons, or confidential commercial information; the confidential part is withheld and the rest given;
- giving access would reveal another person's personal information; it is redacted where it can be separated, and the rest given.

Each Qualification relied on is recorded with the request and its justification, for the Accountability Agent to verify (PR43, PR44, PR45).
