# {{title}}

## Purpose

This procedure sets the duties {{client}} has as a business associate: binding its subcontractors and reporting breaches of unsecured PHI to the covered entities it serves.

## Subcontractors

{{client}} lets a subcontractor create, receive, maintain or transmit ePHI on its behalf only after the subcontractor gives satisfactory written assurances, under 164.314(a), that it will safeguard the information. Subcontractors that handle ePHI are listed in the business associate inventory.

## Subcontractor agreements

Each subcontractor agreement meets the same requirements as a business associate agreement: the subcontractor complies with the Security Rule, binds its own subcontractors, and reports security incidents and breaches of unsecured PHI to {{client}}.

## Notifying the covered entity

After discovering a breach of unsecured PHI, {{client}} notifies the covered entity without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. The notice identifies, as far as possible, each individual whose PHI was involved, and gives the other information the covered entity needs for its own notices, as it becomes available.
