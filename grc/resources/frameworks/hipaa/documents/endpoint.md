# {{title}}

## Purpose

This procedure sets how workstations, devices and media that hold or reach ePHI are used, protected and disposed of.

## Workstation use

Workstations that reach ePHI are used only for authorized work, positioned so screens can't be read by others, and locked when unattended.

## Workstation security

Workstations are managed through [[TODO: the device management tool]]: disk encryption, screen lock, supported operating system and automatic updates.

## Device and media movement

Hardware and media holding ePHI are recorded in the asset inventory with their custodian. Moves into, out of and within facilities are logged. Before equipment is moved, a retrievable copy of any ePHI on it is made.

## Disposal and re-use

ePHI is removed from media before re-use, and media are destroyed or sanitized to [[TODO: NIST SP 800-88]] before disposal. A certificate or record is kept for each.
