# {{title}}

## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}} decides whether an incident is a breach of unsecured PHI and makes the notifications the Breach Notification Rule requires.

## Assessment

Every impermissible use or disclosure of PHI is presumed a breach unless a documented assessment of four factors shows a low probability of compromise: the nature and extent of the PHI, the unauthorized person, whether the PHI was actually acquired or viewed, and how far the risk was mitigated.

## Notifying individuals

As a covered entity, {{client}} notifies affected individuals without unreasonable delay and within 60 calendar days of discovery, in plain language with the required content.

## Notifying the media and HHS

Breaches affecting more than 500 residents of a State or jurisdiction are also notified to prominent media there. HHS is notified within 60 days for breaches affecting 500 or more individuals, and within 60 days of year end for smaller ones.

As a business associate, {{client}} notifies the covered entity without unreasonable delay and within 60 days of discovery, identifying each affected individual where possible. [[TODO: shorter contractual deadline, if any]].

## Law enforcement delay

A notification is delayed when a law enforcement official states it would impede an investigation, for the period the rule allows; oral requests are documented.

## Records

Assessments, notifications and the reasons for any delay are kept for six years, so {{client}} can show it met the rule.
