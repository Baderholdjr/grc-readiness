# {{title}}

## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}} analyzes risks to ePHI and reduces them to a reasonable and appropriate level.

## Inventory

The ePHI inventory lists every system, application, device and vendor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits ePHI, with where the data flows. It is updated when systems change.

## Risk analysis

At least every [[TODO: 12 months]] and after significant changes, {{client}} identifies threats and vulnerabilities for each ePHI asset, assesses current controls, and rates the likelihood and impact of each risk. The analysis covers all ePHI, not a sample.

## Risk management

Each risk above [[TODO: the risk acceptance level]] has a treatment with an owner and a date in the risk management plan. Accepted risks are approved by [[TODO: the security official / executive]] and recorded.

## Records

The analysis, the plan and evidence that the planned measures were implemented are kept for six years.

The owner reviews this document at least every {{doc_review_days}} days and after significant changes.
