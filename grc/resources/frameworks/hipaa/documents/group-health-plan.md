## Purpose

This procedure sets the safeguards {{client}} requires of the sponsor of its group health plan.

## Plan documents

The plan documents require the sponsor to safeguard ePHI it handles for the plan, keep adequate separation between the plan and the sponsor, have its agents agree to safeguards, and report security incidents to the plan.
