## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}} obtains consent for children's data when it offers an information society service directly to a child (Art. 8).

## Ages and parental consent

Where consent is the lawful basis for such a service, a child's own consent is valid only from age 16. Member States may set a lower age, not below 13. {{client}} applies the age of each Member State where it offers the service: [[TODO: countries and the ages that apply]].

Below that age, processing is lawful only if, and to the extent that, consent is given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility. Art. 8 does not affect national contract law, such as rules on whether a child can enter into a contract (Art. 8(3)).

## Verification

{{client}} makes reasonable efforts to verify that consent is given or authorised by the holder of parental responsibility, taking available technology into account (Art. 8(2)). The method is [[TODO: e.g. age gate and parental email confirmation, or payment card check]] and is proportionate to the risk of the processing. Consent records show the age declared, who gave consent, how it was verified and when. Verification collects no more data than it needs.

## Notices for children

Information addressed to children is written in clear and plain language that a child can easily understand (Art. 12(1)). Children and parents can withdraw consent as easily as it was given. Data collected on a child's consent is erased on request, unless an Art. 17(3) exception applies (Art. 17(1)(f)). Processing aimed at children is screened for a DPIA.
