# {{title}}

## Purpose

This procedure sets how {{client}} handles rights requests under Art. 12 to Art. 22.

## Receiving and verifying requests

Requests can arrive by any channel. Whoever receives one sends it to [[TODO: the privacy team]] the same day. If identity is reasonably in doubt, only the information needed to confirm it is requested (Art. 12(6)).

## Deadlines

{{client}} answers without undue delay and within one month of receipt. Where needed because of complexity or the number of requests, the deadline can be extended by two further months, if the person is told of the extension and the reasons within the first month (Art. 12(3)). If {{client}} will not act, it says why within one month and explains the right to complain and to a judicial remedy (Art. 12(4)). Requests are free. Only a manifestly unfounded or excessive request may be refused or charged a reasonable fee, with the reasons recorded (Art. 12(5)).

## Access

The person gets confirmation, a copy of their data and the Art. 15(1)-(2) information. Other people's data is redacted (Art. 15(4)).

## Rectification

Inaccurate data is corrected, and incomplete data completed, without undue delay (Art. 16).

## Erasure

Data is erased without undue delay when an Art. 17(1) ground applies, including at processors and, as they cycle, in backups. It is refused only under an Art. 17(3) exception: freedom of expression, a legal obligation or public task, public health, archiving or research, or legal claims. If the data was made public, reasonable steps are taken to tell other controllers (Art. 17(2)).

## Restriction

When an Art. 18(1) ground applies, such as while accuracy or an objection is checked, the data is only stored, apart from the uses Art. 18(2) allows. The person is told before restriction is lifted.

## Portability

Data the person provided, processed automatically on consent or contract, is supplied in [[TODO: format, e.g. JSON or CSV]] or sent to another controller where technically feasible (Art. 20).

## Objection

Processing based on public task or legitimate interests stops unless {{client}} shows compelling legitimate grounds or legal claims require it. An objection to direct marketing, including related profiling, is always upheld (Art. 21(3)).

## Automated decisions

A person subject to an Art. 22 decision can get human review, give their view and contest it.

## Telling recipients

Recipients are told of any rectification, erasure or restriction unless impossible or disproportionate, and the person learns who they are on request (Art. 19).
