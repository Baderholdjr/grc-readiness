## Handling requests from an authority

- Any request, letter, inspection notice or complaint referral from a supervisory authority goes straight to [[TODO: the privacy lead or DPO]] and [[TODO: legal counsel]], whoever receives it.
- The privacy lead logs it, confirms the deadline the authority has set, and names one person to answer.
- {{client}} answers fully, accurately and on time. If it cannot meet the deadline, it asks the authority for more time before the deadline passes.
- The records of processing are made available to the authority on request (Art. 30(4)).
- Staff do not destroy, change or withhold any information related to the request.
- Where {{client}} acts as a processor, it tells the controller about the request unless the law forbids it.
