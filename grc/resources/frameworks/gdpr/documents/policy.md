# {{title}}

## Purpose

This policy sets how {{client}} processes personal data in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and how it shows that it does (Art. 5(2), Art. 24).

## Scope

The policy applies to all personal data {{client}} processes as a controller, in every system and on paper, and to everyone who works for {{client}}, including contractors. Processing {{client}} carries out as a processor for its customers follows the Processor Obligations Procedure.

## Principles

{{client}} applies the principles of Art. 5(1) to every processing activity:

- **Lawfulness, fairness and transparency:** each purpose has a lawful basis, and people are told how their data is used.
- **Purpose limitation:** data is collected for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes and not used for incompatible ones; a new use is checked against Art. 6(4) first.
- **Data minimisation:** only the data adequate, relevant and necessary for the purpose is collected.
- **Accuracy:** data is kept accurate and up to date; inaccurate data is corrected or erased without delay.
- **Storage limitation:** data that identifies people is kept no longer than needed, following [[TODO: the retention schedule]].
- **Integrity and confidentiality:** data is protected by the Security of Processing Standard.

## Accountability

{{client}} is responsible for, and must be able to demonstrate, compliance. It does this through the records of processing, its policies and procedures, DPIAs, processor contracts, training records and regular compliance reviews. Measures are reviewed and updated as risks change.

Every new or changed system, product or process goes through [[TODO: the privacy review step in the development lifecycle]] before launch.
