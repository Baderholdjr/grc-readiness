# {{title}}

## Purpose

This policy sets {{client}}'s direction for developing, providing and using AI systems responsibly. Top management approves it and is accountable for it.

## Scope

This policy applies to the AI management system scope: {{scope_statement}}

It applies to everyone who designs, builds, operates, procures or oversees those AI systems, including contractors.

## Commitments

{{client}}'s leadership commits to:

1. **Meet applicable requirements** — legal, regulatory, contractual and those of {{framework}}.
2. **Continually improve** the AI management system and how well it works.
3. **Provide the resources** the AIMS needs, including people with the right skills and time.
4. **Set and track AI objectives** that are consistent with this policy (see DOC-OBJECTIVES).
5. **Integrate** AI governance into existing business processes rather than running it alongside them.

## Principles

AI systems in scope must be developed and operated in line with these principles:

| Principle | What it means at {{client}} |
|---|---|
| Accountability | Every AI system has a named owner who answers for its behaviour and outcomes. |
| Human oversight | People can understand, override and stop AI systems where the impact on individuals warrants it. |
| Fairness | We assess and reduce unjustified differences in how systems perform for different groups. |
| Safety and reliability | Systems are tested against their intended use before release and monitored afterwards. |
| Privacy and security | Personal data is used lawfully and minimally; systems are protected against misuse. |
| Transparency | People know when they are interacting with AI and can get an explanation proportionate to the impact. |
