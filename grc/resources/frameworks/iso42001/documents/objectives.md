## Planning

For each objective the plan records what will be done, the resources required, who is responsible, when it will be completed and how results will be evaluated. Progress is reported at management review (DOC-REVIEW).

## Changes to the AIMS

Changes to the AIMS (scope, policies, processes, roles) are planned before they are made:
1. Describe the change, its purpose and its potential consequences.
2. Check that the AIMS remains effective and that resources and responsibilities are reallocated where needed.
3. Approve the change through the normal document approval route (DOC-DOCINFO).
