This procedure sets how top management reviews the AI management system to make sure it remains suitable, adequate and effective.

## Inputs

Each review considers:
1. Status of actions from previous reviews.
2. Changes in external and internal issues relevant to the AIMS (DOC-SCOPE).
3. Changes in the needs and expectations of interested parties.
4. Information on AIMS performance, including trends in:
   - nonconformities and corrective actions;
   - monitoring and measurement results;
   - audit results;
   - progress on AI objectives.
5. Results of AI risk and impact assessments and the status of treatment plans.
6. Opportunities for continual improvement.
7. Adequacy of resources.

## Outputs

The review records decisions on:
- continual improvement opportunities;
- any need for changes to the AIMS, including the AI Policy, objectives and resources;
- actions, each with an owner and due date.

## Records

Minutes, the input pack and the decisions are retained per DOC-DOCINFO. Actions are tracked to completion.
