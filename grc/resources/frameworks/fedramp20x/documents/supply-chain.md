This standard sets how {{client}} identifies, reviews, mitigates and monitors supply chain risk for its cloud service offering, covering suppliers, third-party services, and third-party software. It covers two Key Security Indicators that Class B and C must show. They are not in FedRAMP's mandatory Class A list (FRC-CLA-MFR), so Class A need not show them.

Each indicator is shown with measures and their objectives, run persistently on a known cycle, with machine-readable evidence produced automatically where practical. The measures are summarized in the security decision record (see DOC-FR-PACKAGE).

## Assessing and mitigating supply chain risk

Key Security Indicator: potential supply chain risks are persistently identified, reviewed and mitigated (KSI-SCR-MIT).

Before use, each new supplier, third-party service or significant software component is assessed for:

- the federal customer data or functions it can affect;
- its own security assurance, for example a FedRAMP certification, SOC 2 report or ISO 27001 certificate;
- its location and ownership, where relevant to the risk;
- the contract terms: security duties, notification of vulnerabilities and incidents, and right to audit;
- for software: maintainer health, license, provenance, and whether a software bill of materials (SBOM) is available.

Mitigations include contract clauses, configuration hardening, isolation, a second source, or not using the supplier. The decision and its owner are recorded in [[TODO: supplier risk register]].

Measures: share of suppliers with a current assessment; high-risk suppliers and the status of their mitigations; components without an SBOM or known provenance. Cycle: persistently, on every new supplier or component and with a re-assessment at least [[TODO: cycle]].
