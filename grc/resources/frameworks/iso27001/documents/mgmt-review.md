## Purpose

This procedure sets how top management reviews the information security management system to make sure it remains suitable, adequate and effective.

## Inputs

Each review considers:
1. Status of actions from previous reviews.
2. Changes in external and internal issues relevant to the ISMS (DOC-ISMS-SCOPE).
3. Changes in the needs and expectations of interested parties.
4. Information on ISMS performance, including trends in:
   - nonconformities and corrective actions;
   - monitoring and measurement results;
   - audit results;
   - fulfilment of information security objectives.
5. Feedback from interested parties.
6. Results of risk assessment and the status of the risk treatment plan.
7. Opportunities for continual improvement.

## Outputs

The review records decisions on:
- continual improvement opportunities;
- any need for changes to the ISMS, including the Information Security Policy (DOC-IS-POLICY), objectives and resources;
- actions, each with an owner and due date.

## Records

Minutes, the input pack and the decisions are retained per DOC-IS-DOCINFO. Actions are tracked to completion.
